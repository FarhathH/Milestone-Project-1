Funanza

A new experience begins

It's not just a fun house, it's a fun mansion. An amusement adventure that takes you on a journey through various stages of attractions. From wacky situations to visual deceptions and more to explore. If you wish to come along, invite a friend to share a goofy memory.

Key features to see

Crystal Maze

Step into a neon futuristic maze, where the environment plays with your mind. Is there a way or a dead end? Soak in the vibrant atmosphere as you traverse the halls and immerse yourself in a world where you may need more than your sight to navigate your way.

Ghost Train VR

As you hop onto your destination, explore the derelict compartments as you enter rooms and cabins with the staff waiting on you. Just be warned that you share the train with some paranormal passengers too. Not to worry! They don't bite.

Roingo & Doingo

Meet the dream team duo. They're here to show you the world of Funanza and its' ins and outs. They are always up and about, causing mischief and smiles. Rumours say that they blew up the last circus with an atomic rainbow bomb. They say that's bonkers, but who knows.

Escape Room Mystery

You wake up in a strange urban complex and you see a tv screen with a message: “I need you here”. Who put you in here and where are you? There's no windows, no skylight and not even a signal on your phone. Just clues and an inventory backpack of tools to assist you.
